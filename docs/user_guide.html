Welcome to RCM Dashboard

Your comprehensive Reliability Centered Maintenance tool.

🔐 First Time Setup: Fill out the registration form to activate your 30-day offline trial.
🎬 Intro Video: Click "Start" to watch, or "Skip Intro" to jump straight in.

System Overview

The main dashboard displays your facility's process flow diagram.

🖱️ How to Navigate: Click on any component (e.g., ESDV-101, V-101) to view its specific failure modes and maintenance data.

Component Failure Modes

Drill down into the reliability data for any selected equipment.

  • 📊 Failure Mode Table: View Cause, Category, and Target Reliability R(t).
  • 📋 Task List: See required maintenance tasks and MTBF (Days).
  • 🚦 Status Indicators: Color-coded rows show current health (Green/Yellow/Red).

Time-Travel Analysis

Simulate equipment degradation over time using the Day Slider.

🎚️ Current Day Slider: Located at the bottom of the screen. Drag the slider from Day 1 to Day 365 to see how failure mode statuses and maintenance requirements change dynamically.

Data & System Management

Keep your maintenance logs and system data up to date.

  • 📤 Top Right Menu: Export/Import System Logs and Clear Maintenance records.
  • 👤 Footer Info: View your registered User Name and License Expiry date.
  • ⚠️ Expired License: The app continues to work, but the footer will blink red.
